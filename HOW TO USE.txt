CAPTION IMAGES

New here? Open "Caption Images - Team Guide.pdf" in this folder: a step-by-step
guide with pictures and the common codes.
Ingest photos from a card, cull them, and caption them, with
Photo Mechanic-style code replacements.

---------------------------------------------------------------
START IT
---------------------------------------------------------------
Mac:      Double-click "Caption Images" (the orange app icon)
Windows:  Double-click "Caption Images (Windows).bat"

It opens in its own window (in Chrome or Edge if you have one, otherwise
your normal browser). Opening it again just brings the window back.

Keep the app in this folder, next to "Code Replacements". To keep it
handy, drag it onto your Dock (that makes a shortcut; the app stays here).

To quit: click the power button at the top right, or on a Mac press
Command-Q / right-click the Dock icon > Quit. If you just close the
window, it quits by itself a few minutes later. It never quits in the
middle of copying photos.

FIRST TIME ON A MAC (one time only):
1. If it says Apple "could not verify" Caption Images: click Done, open
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to Caption Images. Then open it again.
2. If it asks to install "Command Line Tools", click Install, wait for
   it to finish, then open Caption Images again.
3. When it asks to access files in your Documents / iCloud Drive, or on
   a removable volume (your memory card), click Allow.
   If you clicked Don't Allow by mistake: System Settings > Privacy &
   Security > Files and Folders > Caption Images, and turn the switches on.

FIRST TIME ON WINDOWS: nothing to install - just double-click "Caption
Images (Windows)". The first time you open it, a black window says
"Setting up Caption Images" for a minute or so while it adds the photo
tools (smaller copies, HEIC previews, jersey-number reading) that don't
come with Python itself. Be online for that. If it can't, the app still
opens and sets them up next time.

The three tabs at the top go in order: Ingest -> Cull -> Caption.
"Open folder..." (top right) opens any folder of photos to cull or caption.

---------------------------------------------------------------
1. INGEST  (copy photos off a card)
---------------------------------------------------------------
- Plug in your card. It shows up under "Copy from" on its own.
- Choose where to save, type the event (e.g. Football vs Toledo).
  A new folder is made, like "2026-11-02 Football vs Toledo".
- File names: keep the camera's names, or rename. The default pattern
  {date}_{event}_{num} gives 20261102_Football-vs-Toledo_0001.cr3.
  Other fill-ins: {isodate} {time} {original} {initials}.
  RAW+JPEG pairs keep the same number.
- Your photographer name is added to every photo.
- "Copy" can leave out your rejects, or copy only photos you rated
  (see "Working on the card" below).
- "Skip photos I've already ingested" means you can ingest the same card
  again after shooting more and only the new photos are copied.
- Ingesting never moves or deletes anything on the card. Format the card
  in your camera once you've checked the copies.
- When it's done, click "Cull these photos".

METADATA TEMPLATE  (bottom of the Ingest page)
- Tick "Add this to every photo" and fill in what you want stamped on
  each photo as it's copied: caption, headline, title, keywords,
  copyright, credit, source, venue, city, state, country, job ID and
  instructions. Leave a field blank to skip it.
- Fill-ins work in every field: {event} {longdate} (November 2, 2026)
  {isodate} {year} {num} {original}. So "{year} Bowling Green State
  University" always has the right year.
- GETTY FORMAT: click "Getty style". It ticks "Getty format" and fills
  the empty boxes: Credit BG Falcon Media, Copyright
  "{photographer}/BG Falcon Media", Source "{photographer}", Bowling
  Green, Ohio, United States, USA, Staff. {photographer} is replaced by
  the photographer name you typed, so each teammate gets their own name
  (e.g. "Coyle Withrow/BG Falcon Media"). Then type the Venue the way it reads in a
  caption ("the Stroh Center") and, in Caption, just what's happening:
    The visiting Cleveland State Vikings and the Bowling Green Falcons
    warm up before a non-conference women's basketball game
  Each photo gets the dateline in front and the date, venue, place and
  your credit at the end, using the photo's own date:
    BOWLING GREEN, OHIO - NOVEMBER 05: The visiting Cleveland State
    Vikings and the Bowling Green Falcons warm up before a non-conference
    women's basketball game on November 5, 2026 at the Stroh Center in
    Bowling Green, Ohio. (Coyle Withrow/BG Falcon Media)
  The box under Caption shows exactly how it will read. Anything you
  already wrote yourself (a dateline, the date, a credit) isn't added
  twice. The photo's Location field gets "Stroh Center" without "the".
- Codes work too, e.g. \bgdate\ or \ut\, using the code files turned
  on in the Caption tab. Unknown codes are flagged in red.
- The caption only goes on photos that don't have one yet (one you wrote
  while culling on the card stays). Keywords are added to any already
  there. The other fields replace whatever the camera wrote.
- "Save as..." keeps the template under a name (e.g. Volleyball home);
  pick it from the menu next time. "Save" updates it after you edit,
  "Delete" removes it. Whatever is in the boxes is remembered either way.
- SHARING TEMPLATES: saved templates are files in the "Metadata
  Templates" folder next to the app (one .json file each). They come
  with the app when you share the zip. To give a teammate one template,
  send them its .json file to drop into their Metadata Templates folder.
- Written the same way as captions: inside JPEGs, in an .xmp sidecar
  for RAW files. Lightroom, Photo Mechanic and Bridge all read it.

---------------------------------------------------------------
WORKING ON THE CARD  (no copying first)
---------------------------------------------------------------
You can cull and caption straight off the card:
- On the Ingest tab, pick the card, then click "Cull on the card" or
  "Caption on the card". (Or "Open folder..." and choose the card.)
  Every folder on the card (100CANON, 101CANON...) shows up together.
- Ratings, captions and your name are saved on the card, in a small .xmp
  file next to each RAW (JPEGs keep them inside the file). The camera
  ignores these files.
- "Move rejects" is turned off on a card. Leave rejects behind instead:
  when you ingest, set "Copy" to "Everything except rejects" or
  "Rated 1 star or more". Ratings and captions come along with the copies.
- Careful: formatting the card erases everything on it, including your
  ratings and captions. Copy your picks off first ("Copy N photos to..."
  in Cull works too).
- If it says the card is locked, slide the lock switch on the side of the
  SD card up and plug it back in.

---------------------------------------------------------------
2. CULL  (pick the keepers)
---------------------------------------------------------------
  Left / Right arrows   previous / next photo
  T                     tag / untag (Photo Mechanic's tag)
  1 - 5                 star rating (moves to the next photo)
  6 - 9                 color class 1-4 (Winner, Winner Alt, Superior,
                        Superior Alt)
  0                     clear the rating
  X                     reject (press again to un-reject)
  Z, Space, or click    zoom to 100% to check focus (Esc to leave)
  G                     contact sheet (grid) / one big photo
  Shift + arrows        select a run of photos (or Shift-click,
                        Cmd/Ctrl-click to add one, Cmd/Ctrl+A for all)
  ?                     every shortcut, on screen

- Number keys can work like any of Photo Mechanic's three keyboard
  presets. Press ? and pick "Number keys set":
    1-5 stars, 6-9 color classes   (Photo Mechanic: "Lightroom Classic")
    1-5 stars only                  ("Numbers set Star Ratings")
    1-8 color classes               ("Numbers set Color Classes")
- With several photos selected, T, X, stars and color classes change
  all of them at once, and "Copy N selected to..." copies just those.
- The big photo is a sharp 2000 px preview (the website size). It shows
  the camera's quick preview first and sharpens a moment later.

- "Show" filters the strip: not rated yet, 3 stars or more, rejected...
- "Copy N photos to..." copies whatever is showing (RAW, JPEG and their
  sidecars) to another folder, e.g. your picks for the editor.
- "Move rejects..." moves rejected photos into a "Rejects" folder inside
  the shoot. Nothing is deleted. (Not available on a card.)
- Ratings are the standard star rating Lightroom, Bridge and Photo
  Mechanic read (a reject is -1, same as Lightroom).
- Stars you give in the camera (the rating button) show up here too, on
  the card and after ingesting. Change them here and your change wins.
- Tags and color classes are saved where Photo Mechanic keeps them
  (its own XMP fields), so a folder can go back and forth between this
  app and Photo Mechanic. Lightroom reads the stars, not tags.

SWITCHING FROM PHOTO MECHANIC
  Photo Mechanic              Here
  Ingest + stationery pad     Ingest tab + Metadata template
  Contact sheet               Cull tab, press G
  Preview window              Cull tab, one big photo (G again)
  T to tag                    T to tag
  Color classes               6-9, or 1-8 with that preset (press ?)
  Show tagged only            Show: Tagged
  Copy tagged photos          Show: Tagged, then "Copy N photos to..."
  Code replacements \code\    Same codes, same files, in Caption
  IPTC info / captions        Caption tab

---------------------------------------------------------------
3. CAPTION
---------------------------------------------------------------
- Type a caption under each photo. It saves on its own after a moment.
- Enter = save and go to the next photo.  Shift+Enter = new line.
- "Show" can limit the list to photos without a caption, or to your
  3-star picks.
- TAGS: under each caption. Type a tag and press Enter (or a comma).
  Click x to remove one. Tags are saved as Keywords, the field Photo
  Mechanic, Lightroom and Bridge search. Click the tag box (or press the
  down arrow) for a list of the players and coaches on the teams that are
  on, grouped by team. Type a number (9 or #9), a name, or a school to
  narrow it; up/down arrows + Return, or a click, picks one. Each team has
  a colour dot in its school colours (also on player tags, suggestions
  and the Teams buttons).
  YOUR TAG LIST: "Tags.txt" (next to the app) holds tags you use often,
  one per line. They show in the tag box's list in every folder. Open it
  in TextEdit (or Notepad), add or delete lines and save; the app picks
  up the change the next time you click a tag box. Lines starting with #
  are notes. The list also includes tags already used in the open folder.
  Player tags (with the person
  icon) are also saved as "Person Shown". Click a player tag to put
  their full ID in the caption (Shift-click for just "Name #9").
- When you're done, click "Move N captioned photos to..." (top right)
  and choose a folder, e.g. one for the editor or for upload. Only photos
  that have a caption go (and only those the "Show" filter is showing).
  Their .xmp sidecars go with them, so nothing is lost.
  Switch "Move" to "Copy" to leave the originals where they are.
  If a file with the same name is already there, nothing is replaced:
  the new one is saved as e.g. IMG_0001-2.
  On a memory card it can only copy (the card is never changed).
- SEND TO LIGHTROOM: set the menu next to that button to
  "Send to Lightroom" and click "Send N captioned photos to Lightroom".
  Like Move and Copy, it sends the captioned photos the "Show" filter is
  showing (e.g. set Show to "Rated 3 stars or more" for your picks).
  Lightroom opens its import window with them picked; choose how to
  import and click Import there. Stars, captions and tags go with them.
  If you have both Lightroom Classic and Lightroom, pick one in the menu
  that appears (it remembers).
  On Windows, Lightroom has to be installed in the usual place
  (Program Files\Adobe), and it takes about 350 photos at a time.
- SMALLER COPIES: set the menu next to that button to
  "Save smaller copies of", pick a size and, if needed, a file-size limit:
    2000 px (web) - 1080 px (social) - 3000 px (editor, print) - Full size
    Any file size - Under 5 MB / 2 MB / 1 MB / 500 KB (for email or
    upload limits; it lowers quality a little, then size, to fit)
  Click "Save N smaller copies to..." and choose a folder. You get JPEGs
  (made from the RAW's full-size preview), turned upright, with the
  caption, photographer, stars, tags, date taken, and the copyright,
  credit and location from the metadata template. The originals
  aren't touched. Works on a memory card too.

---------------------------------------------------------------
TEAMS
---------------------------------------------------------------
When the code files you have on include more than one team, a Teams row
appears on the Caption tab. Open its menu and untick the teams that
aren't in this game ("All off", then tick the two playing, is quickest).
Only players and coaches from the teams that are on can be tagged: typing
the name of someone from a team that's off doesn't tag them, and a tag you
already added for them turns orange as a warning.

(Reading jersey numbers to suggest players is switched off. It named the
wrong players too often. Tag players from the tag box's list instead.)

---------------------------------------------------------------
WHERE THINGS ARE SAVED
---------------------------------------------------------------
- JPEG and PNG: inside the file (the same caption, photographer,
  rating, keyword and Person Shown fields Photo Mechanic, Lightroom and
  Photoshop use).
- RAW (CR3, NEF, ARW...), HEIC and TIFF: in a sidecar file next to it,
  e.g. IMG_0001.xmp for IMG_0001.CR3, the same way Lightroom does it.
  The RAW file itself is never changed. Keep the .xmp files with the RAWs
  when you move or share them.

---------------------------------------------------------------
PHOTOGRAPHER NAME
---------------------------------------------------------------
- Type your name once (Ingest or Caption tab). The app remembers it.
- It's added to photos that don't have a photographer yet. A photo that
  already has someone else's name keeps it; the app never replaces
  another photographer's credit. Each photo shows "By ..." on the
  Caption tab.

---------------------------------------------------------------
CODE REPLACEMENTS
---------------------------------------------------------------
- Code files live in the "Code Replacements" folder next to the app.
  Same format as Photo Mechanic: one code per line, a tab, then the text.
- On the Caption tab, open the Codes menu and tick the code files for
  your shoot (for example Football and DATES). The menu button shows
  which are on.
- Type a code between backslashes and it expands as you type:
      \q1\   ->  in the first quarter
      \doyt\ ->  at Doyt L. Perry Stadium in Bowling Green, Ohio
- COLUMNS: a code can have more than one replacement, separated by tabs
  on the same line (Photo Mechanic's column style). \code\ uses the
  first, \code#2\ the second, \code#3\ the third. Every player and
  coach is one line with three columns:
    ut9  Audrey Dyas #9 of the Toledo Rockets  Audrey Dyas #9  Dyas
      \ut9\    ->  Audrey Dyas #9 of the Toledo Rockets
      \ut9#2\  ->  Audrey Dyas #9
      \ut9#3\  ->  Dyas
    Coaches: \vbalex\ = Head coach Alex Delpiombo of the Bowling Green
    Falcons, \vbalex#2\ = head coach Alex Delpiombo, \vbalex#3\ =
    Delpiombo.
  To add a player, add one line: code, tab, full ID, tab, Name #N, tab,
  last name.
- "Look up codes" searches every code that's on and shows each column
  in its own cell. Click a cell to insert that version.
- Turn on only one sport at a time. Some codes (h1, foul, drive...)
  mean different things in different sports, and opponents use the same
  short school code in every sport (ut = Toledo, ksu = Kent State...).
- The codes follow Getty Images caption style:
    \bgdate\ \nov5\: \vb9\ spikes the ball against the \ut\ \s2\
    \nov5d\ \stroh\ \inbg\ \cwbg\
  becomes
    BOWLING GREEN, OHIO - NOVEMBER 05: Avery Hobson #9 of the Bowling
    Green Falcons spikes the ball against the Toledo Rockets during
    the second set on November 5, 2026 at the Stroh Center in Bowling
    Green, Ohio. (Coyle Withrow/BG Falcon Media)
  Player codes: vb9 = full ID, vb9#2 = "Avery Hobson #9", vb9#3 =
  "Hobson". Opponents the same way: ut9, ut9#2, ut9#3. Dates: nov5 = NOVEMBER 05 (dateline),
  nov5d = on November 5, 2026.
- Every sport has bg = Bowling Green Falcons (like ut = Toledo Rockets),
  plus: agst = against the, gv = A general view of, fans = Fans cheer,
  stu = The Bowling Green student section cheers, cheer = Bowling Green
  cheerleaders perform, band, freddie / frieda (the mascots), and
  sn = is honored during Senior Night (sd = Senior Day for football and
  soccer; hc = during the Homecoming game for football).
- Every sport also has a head coach code: fbcoach, mbbcoach, wbbcoach,
  hkycoach, msoccoach, wsoccoach, vbcoach (\vbcoach#3\ = last name), plus
  pressc = speaks during a postgame press conference, and tournament
  codes (mact, ncaa, mvct for men's soccer, macc and bowl for football).
- New sport files, no rosters yet: Baseball, Softball, Gymnastics,
  Swimming and Diving, Track and Field, Tennis, Golf. Add players the
  same way as the other files.
- PHOTO CREDITS: everyone on the photo team has a credit code in DATES,
  their initials + bg: cwbg = (Coyle Withrow/BG Falcon Media), qdbg =
  (Quentin Dolengowski/BG Falcon Media) and so on. The full list is in
  the team guide. New on the team? Add a line to DATES.txt the same way.
- NEWS: turn on News (and DATES) for news photos around Bowling Green and
  northwest Ohio. Datelines: toldate = TOLEDO, OHIO -, intol = in Toledo,
  Ohio. (every town works the same way: perrydate, inperry...). Places
  (union, jerome, wccourt, tma...), officials (bgmayor = Bowling Green
  Mayor Mike Aspacher, #2 = name, #3 = last name), meetings (council,
  trmeet), elections, public safety, weather, community events and campus
  life. Officials were checked Nov. 5, 2026. In January 2027 switch \gov\ to
  Gov. Amy Acton and \wcclee\ to Commissioner Ryan Lee.
- A full caption from codes:
    \bgdate\ \nov5\: \wbb11\ (left) takes a floater over \csu10\ (right)
    \dncwbb\ \agst\ \csu\ \nov5d\ \stroh\ \inbg\ \cwbg\
  (cwbg is Coyle's credit; teammates add their own code the same way)
